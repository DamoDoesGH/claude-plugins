## IDEA

**Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise? How do you know people need what you're making?**

I have done this once. Founders Pledge is one document signed at formation; 2,300 founders have put $13.6bn through it. Liquid is the same document for shares.

Illiquidity hurts all market participants. Founders give more equity per hire because option grants lose to public RSUs. Employees hold equity divorced from value: 16,538 sold into tenders in 2025, out of a million on Carta. LPs believe in DPI, not IRR. Every one of them wants the same thing, and the founder is the only one who can say yes.

**Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?**

Forge, Hiive, Nasdaq Private Market, Carta, SecondMarket.

All of them fought for the same 200 names and asked for consent one trade at a time. SecondMarket skipped it and Facebook shut it down. Forge cleared $17bn in twelve years against $9.4tn. Carta was administrator and broker at once.

Consent is free at formation and nobody has asked for it there. That is the whole business.

**How do or will you make money? How much could you make?**

Sellers pay 2% of cash cleared. Companies pay nothing to sign; $200 a month warrants their numbers, $12,000 a year books two auctions. Year three: 1,200 auctions at a $4m median is $96m, plus $30m in subscriptions and data.

Venture is a small asset class because it barely trades. 2% of its value changes hands a year, $188bn. Houses turn over 4.4%. At that rate the market is $414bn.
